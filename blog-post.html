What to expect at your first psychiatric visit

Sample post · 4 min read

Booking a first psychiatric visit can feel like a big step. Knowing what happens can make it easier.

How long it takes

Most first visits run about an hour. There's time to talk, and nothing is rushed.

What we talk about

  • What's been going on, and for how long
  • Your health history and any medications
  • Sleep, stress, work and relationships
  • What you'd like to feel different

What to bring

A list of your current medications and doses, your insurance card, and any questions you want to ask.

What happens next

We agree on a plan together. That might include medication, therapy, lifestyle changes, or a mix. Follow-up visits are shorter and focus on how things are going.
